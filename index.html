CN-GS: Curve-Constrained Gaussian Splatting for Netting-Preserving Scene Reconstruction

Abstract

We propose CN-GS, a Gaussian Splatting method tailored to netting scene reconstruction. It first augments the sparse SfM initialization points with grid-sampled points, then uses Edge Masks to provide per image supervision and Bézier curves to impose 3D geometric constraints, guiding Gaussian primitives to reconstruct netting structures. We further propose a novel regularization strategy to improve Gaussians' geometric consistency. Our framework enables disentangled modeling of netting and remaining regions without relying on pretrained models such as Segment Anything.

NettingScenes

We introduce NettingScenes, a dataset designed specifically for netting scene reconstruction. It comprises 11 scenes spanning real-world captures and synthetic environments created using Habitat-Sim and Blender.
